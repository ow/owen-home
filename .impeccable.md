## Design Context

### Users
Product and design leaders, senior practitioners, and potential collaborators reviewing Owen's work. They are looking for concise evidence of leadership, craft, and an ability to make deeply technical product spaces understandable while building systems that give teams leverage.

### Brand Personality
Candid, technically curious, and quietly opinionated. The writing should feel like Owen explaining the work to a smart peer: first-person, specific, and human without becoming promotional or overly polished.

### Aesthetic Direction
A dark, code-adjacent portfolio with animated ASCII texture, cool blue accents, and focused editorial layouts. New work should feel native to the existing site rather than introduce a new visual system.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the problem and the change the work made.
- Show leadership through the conditions, systems, and leverage created for teams—not only the artifact that shipped.
- Prefer real details and plain language over portfolio jargon.
- Keep case studies compact enough to scan, with optional rich media for depth.
- Make technical complexity approachable without sanding away the interesting parts.
- Extend existing patterns before adding new ones.
